Desi Fitness (“the app”) is published by Muhammad Badar Hayat. This policy explains what information the app collects and how it is used. Contact: yasmeenaziz016@gmail.com.

Information we collect

When you create an account and use Desi Fitness, you may provide:

  • Account details: username and display name.
  • Body and fitness details you enter: height, weight, meals, nutrition, steps, exercise, calorie targets, and fasting sessions.
  • App preferences: language (Roman Urdu or Urdu) and timezone offset from your device, used to show the correct local date.

The Android app is a secure wrapper around the Desi Fitness website. It does not read your contacts, photos, location, or body sensors. Health numbers in the app are values you type in, not measurements from your phone.

How we use information

We use this information only to operate Desi Fitness: log you in, save your tracking history, calculate calorie and body-metric summaries, and remember your language. We do not sell your data. We do not show third-party ads in the app today.

Where data is stored

Your account data is stored on servers used to host Desi Fitness (currently PythonAnywhere). Login uses a username stored in a session cookie. Keep your username private. Session cookies and timezone cookies stay on your device so the app can keep you signed in.

Services used to run the app

  • Hosting for the website and saved user data.
  • Google Fonts, which may receive your IP address when fonts load.
  • Google Play and Android, which may collect device and install information under Google’s own policies.

Children

Desi Fitness is not directed at children under 13. Do not create an account for a child under 13.

Health disclaimer

Desi Fitness is a personal food and fitness tracker for desi meals. It is not medical advice, a diagnostic tool, or a substitute for a doctor or dietitian.

Access, correction, and deletion

You can update your name and height in the app. To request deletion of your account and saved logs, email yasmeenaziz016@gmail.com from a message that includes your username.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page. Continued use of the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
